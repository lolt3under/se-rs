# Code of conduct

This project is maintained in public. Discussion can be blunt about code and
still be decent toward the person who wrote it.

## Expected conduct

- Keep criticism about a patch, claim, benchmark, or design decision.
- Back technical objections with a failing input, documentation, or a measured
  result when one is available.
- Give people room to correct mistakes.
- Respect names, identities, backgrounds, and levels of experience.
- Stop a line of discussion when a maintainer asks for a pause or moves it to a
  more appropriate issue.

Harassment, threats, sexual attention, deliberate misgendering, publication of
private information, and sustained personal attacks are not accepted here.
Neither is using project spaces to recruit people into unrelated disputes.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other
spaces represented as part of the project. Report a problem privately through
the repository's security contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may
edit or remove comments, lock a thread, reject a contribution, or block an
account. The response will depend on severity and whether the behavior repeats.

Maintainers must disclose a conflict of interest and hand a report to another
maintainer when possible. Reports and the identity of a reporter are kept
private except where disclosure is required by law or needed to prevent harm.
